Staking rewards, not your principal, pay for [private inference](/cloud/private-inference) on NEAR AI Cloud. Use this calculator to translate between a monthly inference budget and the NEAR you'd need to stake to cover it, or start from an amount of NEAR and see what it buys.

Everything below is a live model: it fetches the current NEAR price on load, defaults to the network's current staking APY, and lets you override either assumption to see how the numbers move. See [Staking for Inference](/cloud/staking-for-inference/overview) for how the underlying mechanism works.

      <div class="explain">
        <h3>The formula</h3>
        <pre>monthly AI usage covered = staked NEAR × NEAR price × APY ÷ 12
stake needed             = monthly AI usage × 12 ÷ (NEAR price × APY)</pre>
        <p>Rewards accrue continuously, so a long-running workload can also read it per second: <code>staked NEAR × price × APY ÷ 31,557,600</code>.</p>

        <h3>Why the reward rate moves</h3>
        <p>NEAR issues a fixed amount of new token each year and splits it among everyone staking. Your share depends on how much of the supply is staked network-wide: when less is staked, the same rewards divide among fewer tokens and the rate rises; when more is staked, it falls. In the limit where everything is staked, the reward rate converges on the network's inflation rate — nobody gains on anybody. Validator commission, typically 1–10%, comes out of that.</p>
        <p>So your covered usage drifts with both the NEAR price and the network's staking share. It is a subsidy that floats, not a fixed plan.</p>

        <h3>What a credit is</h3>
        <p>One credit is one US dollar of usage on NEAR AI Cloud — the same unit your bill is in. Rewards convert to credits automatically, nothing is sold, and unused credits carry forward.</p>
